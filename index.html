Researcher's judgment Verdict, not a finding of the report

The thesis is right about where things end up and early on timing. Over 5 to 10 years the software layer (Steam, SteamOS, Proton, Windows on Xbox) becomes the product. Over the next 12 months the evidence runs the other way: exclusives are returning, kernel anti-cheat still blocks the biggest competitive shooters on Linux (BF6, CoD, PUBG, R6 Siege are Borked, while CS2 and Marvel Rivals are Gold and ARC Raiders is Platinum), Linux share fell to 3.05% in September, and Sony is the one ending discs. Xbox has not decided on a Helix disc drive.

Six parts of the thesis, against the record

The packet's section C sets out counter-evidence for each part. Open any part to see it. The packet calls exclusivity the strongest counter.

"Console exclusivity is dying"strongest counter
  • Sony: narrative single-player first-party games are now PlayStation-exclusive, per Hulst's town hall as reported by Schreier on 18 May 2026. Live-service and multiplayer games stay on PC (Marathon is about 70% PC, per an analyst estimate estimate).
  • Xbox: Gears of War: E-Day and Clockwork Revolution are permanent Xbox console exclusives, decided "case-by-case" (9 Jun 2026). Halo: Campaign Evolved and Fable stay multiplatform.
  • Reported reasoning: weaker PC sales for sequels (Alinea), plus the need for console-selling reasons as hardware prices climb.
  • GTA 6, the year's biggest launch, is console-only: 19 Nov 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, no PC version at launch. Rockstar newswire snippet only
"Proton is near-solved for AAA"not on this record
  • ProtonDB lists Battlefield 6, Call of Duty, PUBG and Rainbow Six Siege as Borked, with Apex trending Bronze (API pull, 21 Oct 2026). Explore the tiers in the next section.
  • AreWeAntiCheatYet: 196 supported (17%), 278 running (24%), 638 broken (55%), 53 denied (5%). Its Denied list includes Fortnite, Apex, Valorant, R6 Siege and BF2042.
  • Only 46% of the Steam top 100 and 36% of the top 1000 are Steam Deck Verified or Playable (2 Sep 2026). That is Deck-specific and stricter than "runs on Linux."
  • The hopeful sign cuts the other way for SteamOS: Epic's "EAC Linux v2" (14 Oct 2026) needs a signed kernel and modules plus secure boot with attestation, which Steam Deck doesn't currently support. No date given. The packet reads this as pointing to a split where only locked-down Linux gets competitive games report's own inference
  • An EA Javelin job listing (Mar 2026) mentioned future Linux/Proton support. Not a commitment; BF6 is still blocked.
  • A community guide puts 70 to 80% of the most-played Steam games at Silver or better and 10 to 15% Borked. The packet treats it as a low-quality secondary source, because it wrongly lists Fortnite as using BattlEye.
"Linux is the key"not on this record
  • Sep 2026 Steam survey: Linux 3.05% (down 0.85 pts), Windows 95.03%, macOS 1.92%. GamingOnLinux ties the drop to a rise in Simplified Chinese. Top distros: CachyOS 18.12%, SteamOS Holo 17.37% (down 3.70). August was 3.90%, implied by the same article. The October survey isn't out yet.
  • Newzoo has PC growth slowing to 5.3% in 2026 (from 12% in 2025); mobile has 57% share.
  • Steam Deck sales fell about 80% after the May price rise, from roughly 11k to 18k a week in 2025 to roughly 1.4k to 3k a week chart-position estimate
  • Steam Machine: about 12k to 15k units a week (range 8.7k to 15.6k) as of 18 Jul, or about 600k to 750k a year, supply-bound napkin math, Boiling Steam
  • Microsoft's counter: Project Helix "plays your Xbox console and PC games," Xbox mode on Windows 11, and more than 1,500 Play Anywhere games.
"Underpowered Steam Machine hardware doesn't matter; software is the product"not on this record
  • Price $1,049 (512 GB) or $1,349 (2 TB); $1,128 / $1,428 with a controller. Valve blames RAM and storage costs.
  • Tom's Hardware (22 Jun 2026) calls the hardware "already aging," not always 4K, and says a PS5 or Xbox plus a Switch 2 together cost less. Polygon's roundup: "curb your expectations." Test that claim against the packet's October prices further down.
  • Specs: semi-custom AMD Zen 4 CPU and RDNA 3 GPU, 16 GB DDR5 plus 8 GB GDDR6, "over six times" the Steam Deck. From Pogwire's summary; Valve's own spec page was not fetched.
  • X posts argue jailbroken PS5s now undercut its value X post, signal
  • The packet's strongest form of this counter: the software (Proton) is what fails on the live-service games that drive engagement.
"Nintendo is the last physical holdout under pricing pressure"half right
  • Price pressure is real: Switch 2 US price $449.99 to $499.99 on 1 Sep 2026; Japan ¥49,980 to ¥59,980; about a ¥100B hit from memory costs and "tariff measures"; FY3/27 forecast 16.5M, down from 19.86M.
  • Physical is holding: 38.5% of software sales were physical in Apr to Jun 2026; US physical software spending up 5% to $738M; US Game-Key Card spending up 631% YTD; Switch 2 led US hardware in units and dollars for August and YTD (Circana, 8 Oct).
  • Nuance from the packet: much of that "physical" is Game-Key Cards, which are really digital downloads report's own inference
  • Xbox, not Nintendo, may be the other physical holdout report's own inference
"Xbox is going all-digital"not supported
  • Microsoft has not decided whether Helix gets a disc drive and surveyed Insiders about it (22 Sep 2026).
  • It launched Disc-to-Digital in Aug 2026 snippet only and still sells the $799.99 Series X with a disc drive.
  • The company actually committed to ending discs is Sony: physical disc production for new PlayStation games ends in January 2028 snippet only, corroborated by Polygon and TechRadar
